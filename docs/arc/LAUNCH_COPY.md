## LinkedIn / long launch post after verification

We built Grey Finance because payroll should not be a monthly reconciliation exercise split across spreadsheets, HR tools, wallets and screenshots.

Grey gives an employer one operating system for company records and Arc-native USDC payroll. Eligible obligations become an immutable payment plan. The employer reviews every recipient and amount, then signs in their own wallet. Grey independently verifies the chain, canonical contract runtime, original run ID, exact events and finalized receipt before anything becomes paid in the company ledger.

The security boundary is simple: no AI model, background worker or server ever receives a treasury key.

Before Mainnet we completed the full Arc Testnet lifecycle — deposit, two-recipient atomic payroll, withdrawal and replay rejection — with finalized public evidence. The Mainnet deployment is separately verified against the exact compiled creation and runtime bytecode.
