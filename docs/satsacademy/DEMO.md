# Three-minute demonstration

**0:00–0:25 — A real problem.** Open the landing page and introduce a first-time Bitcoin learner in Nigeria or West Africa. Explain the gap between knowing payment vocabulary and being able to check a payment safely. Point to the three quests and the hands-on wallet lab.

**0:25–0:55 — Learn something useful.** Start Bitcoin, in everyday words. Change the sats converter and switch to Hausa. Explain that one Bitcoin has 100 million whole sats. Finish the three-question check and show the knowledge achievement.

**0:55–1:20 — Save progress.** Sign in with a Nostr signer and set the profile language. Explain the optional temporary identity honestly: an actual signed identity for trying the app, whose signing key stays only in this tab's memory.

**1:20–2:10 — Verify a wallet operation.** Open Wallet lab. Connect the receiving Alice wallet from the private regtest lab, with no monetary value, or a restricted external NWC wallet. Show invoice creation and lookup permissions. Create a 10-sat invoice, then pay it from Bob on the same network. Before settlement, show that the practical badge is locked.

Select **Check payment status**. Show the settled state, matching payment hash, network, amount, and newly earned Lightning Explorer. Explain that the server checks receiving-wallet status and the preimage/hash; the browser cannot declare it paid.

**2:10–2:40 — Make the evidence useful.** Open the receipt history, copy its full hash, ask the guide why pending is different from paid, and show the source label. Download the complete learning record. Explain that lesson reset retains verified wallet evidence and that the learning wallet is separate from the company treasury.

**2:40–3:00 — Impact and next steps.** Name the metrics and remaining work. Hausa needs native-speaker review; independent learner testing is next. Builder tests and regtest receipts are not adoption or mainnet evidence.

## Metrics to collect with consent

- Learners starting and completing each quest.
- Median time from beginning a lesson to passing its check.
- Percentage creating an invoice and independently verifying settlement.
- Time to the first verified exercise.
- Wallet/signer failures and recovery success without secrets or personal data in logs.
- A short before/after understanding check: invoice versus settlement, and private versus shareable data.
- Task success on mid-range phones and weak connections.

## Next iteration

Review Hausa with native speakers, test with at least five independent learners, improve mobile signer compatibility, and add French after validation. Add earning incentives only after a separate fraud-resistance and operating-cost design.
