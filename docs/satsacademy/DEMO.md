# Three-minute demonstration

0:00–0:25 — Show the primary user: a new learner who understands mobile payments but cannot yet tell a Lightning request from proof of payment. State the local problem: theoretical Bitcoin lessons do not establish practical wallet skills.

0:25–1:00 — Open the first lesson, change a sats amount in the interactive converter, switch to Hausa, and complete a checked quiz. Show that the knowledge badge records a completed lesson, not a transaction.

1:00–1:35 — Open Wallet lab. Connect the included local regtest wallet with clearly visible no-value status, or a capped external NWC wallet. Create a 10-sat receiving invoice. Show pending state and the absence of a verified badge.

1:35–2:10 — Pay the invoice from the second wallet on the same network. Select Verify payment. Show the settled status, matching payment hash, and the newly earned Lightning Explorer badge.

2:10–2:40 — Open a practice receipt and ask the guide, “Why is pending different from paid?” Show whether the response is local course guidance or a configured AI tutor. Show the narrow wallet permissions and explain how to revoke them.

2:40–3:00 — State what is measured and what is next: completion rate, verified exercise rate and time to first independently verified payment. Independent learner testing and native Hausa review are the next iteration; builder tests are not adoption evidence.

## Metrics to collect with consent

- Learners starting and completing each lesson.
- Median time from lesson start to passing the knowledge check.
- Percentage who generate an invoice and independently verify settlement.
- Wallet connection failure reasons, stored without credentials or payment secrets.
- Short pre/post understanding checks: invoice versus settlement, private versus shareable data.
- Reading and task success on mid-range phones and weak connections.

## Next iteration

Review Hausa with native speakers, test with at least five independent learners, improve signer compatibility on mobile, add French content after validation, and add optional offline caching of lessons. Do not add cash rewards before fraud resistance and operating costs have been considered.
